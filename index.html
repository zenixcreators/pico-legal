    Privacy Policy

    Pico keeps your money data on your phone. We have no servers, accounts, ads, or analytics, so we never receive your data.

    Effective November 8, 2026. Applies to Pico version 1.0.1 (com.pico.app) for Android.

    Pico at a glance

    • No account, email, or phone number needed
    • Your expenses, budgets, and settings stay on your device
    • No ads, no analytics, no crash tracking
    • Receipt scanning and spending tips run on your phone
    • Erase everything with one tap in Settings

    Who we are

    Pico is a personal expense and budget tracker published by Haneef. For any privacy question, write to zenixcreators@gmail.com.

    Notification access

    Pico can notice payments for you if you turn on Android’s notification access for it. This is optional.

    • What it reads: notification text from a fixed list of banking and payment apps, such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, CRED, HDFC, SBI, and ICICI, to find the amount, merchant, and payment details.
    • What it ignores: social and chat apps such as WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram are blocked.
    • Where it happens: entirely on your phone. Notification text is never uploaded.
    • Turning it off: switch it off in Android Settings, under notification access. Manual entry and imports keep working.

    What Pico does not do

    • It does not create an account or ask for your email, phone number, or social sign-in.
    • It does not connect to your bank accounts, UPI, or data aggregators.
    • It does not ask for your location, contacts, or microphone, and it does not use an advertising ID.
    • It does not use ad networks, analytics tools, or crash reporting services.
    • It does not sell or share your information.
    • It does not send your data to cloud AI. Spending tips come from simple calculations on your phone.

    Third-party components

    Pico is built with open-source libraries and uses Google ML Kit text recognition to read receipts. These run inside the app on your phone. Pico does not send your receipt images to any server for this.

    Your choices

    • Delete one item: remove any transaction or category yourself.
    • Delete everything: Profile, then Delete all data. This wipes transactions, categories, and settings immediately.
    • Take a copy: export your transactions as CSV or TXT. Exported files are saved where you choose, and Pico can’t control copies you share.
    • Stop reading notifications: turn off notification access at any time.
    • Uninstall: removing Pico removes the data it stored on your phone.

    Because we don’t hold any of your information, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, or delete on our side. You are in control of it on your device.

    Backups

    Pico turns off Android cloud backup for its data. If you reinstall or switch phones, your data will not come back automatically, so export it first if you want to keep it.

    Children

    Pico is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children.

    Changes to this policy

    If Pico ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top, and ask for your permission where the law requires it.

    Data Security

    The strongest protection in Pico is that your data never leaves your phone. This page explains what protects it, and where the limits are.

    Effective November 8, 2026. Applies to Pico version 1.0.1 for Android.

    Security at a glance

    • No Pico server or database exists to be breached
    • PIN stored hashed with a salt in Android’s secure storage
    • Fingerprint and face unlock handled by Android itself
    • Pico locks the moment you leave the app
    • Android cloud backup of Pico’s data is switched off

    What protects your data

    AreaHow it is protected
    Where data livesTransactions and settings stay in Pico’s private app storage. Other apps can’t read it on a standard, unrooted phone.
    App lock PINA 4 to 8 digit PIN is mixed with a random salt and hashed (SHA-256, 1,000 rounds), then kept in Android Keystore backed secure storage. It is never saved as plain text.
    BiometricsAndroid checks your fingerprint or face. Pico never sees or stores biometric data.
    Guessing limitsAfter 5 wrong PINs, waits grow longer, up to 5 minutes. The wait survives closing and reopening the app.
    Auto lockPico locks as soon as it goes to the background.
    Payment notificationsOnly a fixed list of banking and payment apps is read. Social and chat apps are blocked.
    Troubleshooting logKeeps the last 40 entries, with amounts, names, and account numbers removed.
    Receipt scanningDone on your phone. Images are not uploaded.
    BackupsAndroid cloud and device-transfer backups are off for Pico’s data, so it isn’t copied to cloud storage.

    The honest limits

    Transactions are not separately encrypted. They are protected by Android’s app isolation and your phone’s screen lock, but Pico does not add its own encryption on top. Only the PIN is stored in Android’s encrypted secure storage.

    Screenshots are allowed. Pico does not block screenshots, screen recording, or the recent apps preview. Turn on Hide amounts in Profile to mask figures on screen.

    Exports are plain files. CSV and TXT exports are not encrypted. Choose where you save or send them with care.

    A compromised phone is a risk. If someone can unlock your phone, or it is rooted or infected, they may be able to reach Pico’s data. Keep a screen lock on, and use Pico’s app lock too.

    Controls you have

    • Turn app lock on or off, and change your PIN
    • Turn fingerprint or face unlock on or off
    • Hide amounts across the app
    • Turn automatic payment tracking on or off through Android’s notification access
    • Export your data as CSV or TXT
    • Delete all data in one tap, effective immediately
